## What's new in 0.9.2

- **One assistant to talk to.** The new **Front desk** team is an Assistant that answers quick questions itself and hands bigger work to the right team's lead, then tells you how it went. Teams you add later join it on their own, and setup adds it when you pick two goals or more.
- **App studio.** A starter team that builds apps: a Project manager you talk to hands the work to a Researcher, Designer, Coder, Tester, Reviewer and Data scout. Apps it builds open in McBot's Browser. Setup's "Apps & software" goal uses it.
- **Council.** A Chair gives one question to a Skeptic, a Pragmatist and an Expert, then weighs their answers. Best with a different model on each advisor (the agent's page → Thinks with).
- **Ask McBot builds the team you describe.** Every role you name is in the plan, a request to build an app or a CRM gets a builders' team (never a sales agent), and the first agent is the one you talk to. "Quick plan" says on hover why your model didn't plan it.
- **Chat points to the lead.** Talking to an agent that only takes work from others now shows "Works alone · Ask <lead>", one click from the agent that gets the whole team working.
- Fixed: with more than one board, compiling one board deleted another board's agent files, and compiling that board again failed with "profiles/<agent> already exists … not created by McBot" (and "The board has errors" on its runs). Every board now keeps its own agents; folders left behind by the old behaviour work again, with their memory. When two boards want the same agent id, the second one gets its own ("writer-2").
- Fixed: memory, cost, Files, tasks, keys and live sync with Hermes looked only at the board compiled last.
